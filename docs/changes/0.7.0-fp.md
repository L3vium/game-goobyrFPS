# 0.7.0 — first person (track B, agent fp)

## Changelog (player-facing)
- First-person hands are authored around every weapon: both hands wrap the real grips (support hand
  round the firing hand on pistols, on the fore-end, pump or vertical grip on long guns), trigger
  finger on the trigger when aiming and along the frame otherwise, thumbs forward.
- Aiming puts the sights exactly on the screen centre, for every gun.
- Rook's own arms: dark leather gloves (thumb, trigger finger and a three-finger mitt) under the olive
  MA-1 bomber sleeve with its rib cuff, the same glove as the third-person cast.
- Pistol: the support hand wraps the firing hand (fingers over the firing fingers under the guard, both
  thumbs forward along the frame), the wrists in a V under the gun, the sleeves below it.
- The trigger finger is on the trigger when aiming, on every gun.
- Fists up in a guard by the chin; the jab and the cross pull back, then drive to the middle of the
  screen at eye level, turning palm-down, the other fist kept up.
- The punch: left click alternates a jab and a cross with anticipation and a little overshoot; the hit
  lands on contact, not on the click, with a freeze of the arms, a jolt of the view and a squash of
  whoever you hit. Holding something, you throw a quick left jab. Takedowns grab and twist.
- Throwing: an overhand throw, the item leaves the hand at the release.
- Recoil kicks about the sights instead of swinging the gun off-line.

## Design and architecture notes (for Game.md)
- **Weapon points.** Every gun model (W_Pistol, W_SilencedPistol, W_Smg, W_Shotgun, W_Sniper,
  W_RocketLauncher, W_Flamethrower) carries, besides Grip and Muzzle: `Sight` (rear end of the sight
  line, on the bore's vertical plane, its forward along the barrel), `ForeGrip` (centre of what the
  support hand holds) and `MagWell` (where the magazine goes in, +Z up the insertion axis). Pistols
  and the SMG keep their magazine as a child mesh `Mag` (pivot on the MagWell). Source of truth:
  `Tools/Blender/props/build_weapons.py` (and `build_polyhaven.py` for the sniper, whose current export
  got its points by an FBX round trip, `weapon_points.py`). Builds can export to a staging folder
  (`GOOBYR_PROPS_OUT`) that is copied into Assets under the editor lock.
- **Authored first person** (`Tools/Blender/fp`): a generic rig rooted at the eye (the camera),
  shoulders 0.29 m below and 0.13 m behind it, upper arm 0.30, forearm 0.265, two forearm twist bones
  (40 % / 75 % of the hand's roll), the socket `weapon_r` under `hand_r` and `prop_l` under `hand_l`.
  Poses are made weapon-first: the gun is placed by its Sight (on the camera axis when aimed), the
  hands are put on the real handle / fore-end geometry (a cylinder or a hand frame in the gun's
  frame), the arms reach by two-bone IK (elbow towards a pole, hinge-consistent forearm roll), the
  fingers close joint by joint until their pads touch the gun's mesh (BVH), the trigger finger is
  solved onto the trigger face, thumbs reach target points; the socket is keyed equal to the gun's
  Grip frame. Families: Pistol, Smg, Shotgun, Sniper, Launcher, Flamer, Pinch (coin, cards, notes,
  vials), Palm (rock, brick, C4, smoke), Handle (wrench, crowbar, bottle), Fists - each with Hip, ADS
  and Sprint. Clips are keyed hand paths (Catmull-Rom arcs, anticipation, overshoot, settle) solved and
  baked at 60 fps: Jab, Cross, Takedown, and a throw per item family. `fp_poses.json` lists the
  socket and Sight of every pose in camera space and the clip events (strike, release).
- **The arms mesh is the cast's** (`Tools/Blender/characters/cast/glove.py`): `fp_rig.py` builds the
  hand and finger bones from `bone_segments(frame_from_arm(...))` and the mesh from `make_fp_arm`
  (glove, MA-1 sleeve, rib cuff; no armband, the upper arm is out of view), collapse-decimated per part
  (12k / 5k / 2.5k triangles budget: about 39k vertices for both arms, from 370k), its `lowerarm` weight
  spread down the two twist bones, baked with Rook's colours (cast_specs ROOK_LOOKS, linearised) into
  one PBR set. The solver's finger and thumb radii come from the module's `FINGER_R` x `SCALE` (1.10).
  `GOOBYR_FP_PLACEHOLDER=1` (or no cast module) builds the old placeholder glove, same spec.
  First person scales the glove to 0.97 (`GOOBYR_FP_HAND_SCALE`; the cast's own 1.10 'readable' hand
  crowds the view a hand-width from the eye): `glove.SCALE` and `LAYOUT` are overridden in the build
  process only, so bones, mesh and weights agree.
- **Grips.** The firing hand is slid along its handle once per gun (`trigger_fit`: a coordinate search
  along the thumb side, then the fingers) until the trigger finger's pad reaches the trigger face, and
  keeps that grip in every pose of the gun. The pistol support hand is a cylinder grip up the raked front
  strap (its knuckle line along the grip), so its fingers wrap the firing fingers; both thumbs reach
  forward along the frame; the ADS poles put the elbows down and out (wrists in a V under the gun).
  The shotgun's `Sight` sits on a comb line (24 mm over the receiver, tilted 2 deg onto the bead): the
  pose squares the sight line, not the bore, with the view, so the stock and the firing hand sit low;
  the firing hand is also rolled 25 deg out about its fingers and the eye sits 0.20 m behind the sight,
  so the glove stays in the lower-right corner. The launcher's support hand holds the tube from below
  at the fore-grip, fingers closed round its side. The `GRIPS` numbers carry the `trigger_fit` moves
  (the search now starts on the trigger); the pistol support thumb lies on the frame within 3 mm.
- **Size.** Clips are baked at 30 fps with the FBX exporter's key reduction (simplify 0.25, far under the
  1 mm test tolerance).
- **Unity side** (`Goobyr/Build/First Person Arms`, `Editor/Pipeline/FpArmsBuilder.cs`): FP_Arms.fbx
  (`Art/FirstPerson/FP_Arms`) imported as a generic rig, one clip per take, animation compression off,
  its baked colour map forced to sRGB (the shared PBR importer reads `_arm` in the name as a packed map);
  the arms controller (`Art/Resources/FirstPerson/FP_Arms.controller`): layer Pose (a direct blend of
  Hip / ADS / Sprint, weights from code), layer Left (left-arm transform mask: the jab thrown while the
  right hand holds something), layer Action (jab, cross, takedown, reload, throw); one override
  controller per family; `ViewmodelArms` (Resources/FirstPerson) ties model, controllers and the
  manifest together and maps items to families.
- **Viewmodel** (`Scripts/Weapons/Viewmodel.cs`), no IK: the held model is parented to `weapon_r` at
  identity (Grip on the socket). Code adds sway (look and movement), bob, the switch drop, procedural
  reloads for the families without an authored one, recoil rotating the rig about the Sight (the sight
  stays put, the muzzle flips), and a LateUpdate correction that squares the sight line with the view
  and slides it onto the screen centre in proportion to the aim blend. Authored reloads hide the gun's
  `Mag` at magOut (a pistol's falls away), show a copy on `prop_l` from magGrab, and give it back to
  the gun at magIn. The overlay camera draws at 48 deg vertical (`CameraRig.VIEW_FOV`, the FOV the poses
  are authored for), post-processing on, near clip 0.06.
- **Punch timing and networking** (`WeaponHolder`): the owner plays the clip at once and tells the
  others to swing (third-person `Punch` trigger in step with the clip), then resolves at the clip's
  contact time (manifest `strike`, 0.16 s jab / 0.18 s cross / 0.30 s takedown grab): a predicted local
  hit freezes the arms Animator for 70 ms (never `Time.timeScale`), jolts the camera (`CameraRig.Kick`,
  a spring), squashes the victim's body (Feel) and plays the punch on contact; then `MeleeRpc`, which the
  host checks like `FireRpc` (minimum interval 0.3 s between swings, reported eye more than 4 m from the
  body replaced by the real eye) and judges (punch or takedown) as before. `PunchFeedbackRpc` carries the
  victim so every machine squashes it; the owner skips what it already predicted. Bare hands alternate
  jab and cross; holding something, a left jab; an unaware NPC from behind (same test as the host,
  from networked awareness and state) gets the takedown clip, the held item put away during it.
- **Throws** leave the hand at the clip's release (0.27 s); a quick throw of a gadget not in hand goes
  at once.
- **Tests and review**: `FpPoseTests` (EditMode) samples every pose of fp_poses.json from the imported
  clips and checks the socket (1 mm, 0.2 deg) and, with the family's gun in the socket, the Sight
  (1 mm; aimed poses on the camera axis). `Goobyr/Test/First Person Shots (San Lazaro)` renders the
  review set (hip and ADS per gun, fists, jab, cross, takedown, both reloads, throwable held and
  thrown, small items) at 16:9 and 21:9 into Logs/fp_shots.
