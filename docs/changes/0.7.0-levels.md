# 0.7.0 — levels (map agent)

## Changelog (player-facing)
- Sentry guns guard each map's restricted cores (San Lázaro: the vault and the Don's private wing;
  Qasr al-Rimal: the keep's vault and armoury, the war room and the governor's study; Weisshorn: the
  VIP floor and the observatory bunker). Each group answers to a control PC hidden 15-40 m away; a
  thick yellow cable runs from the guns along walls and ceilings, through rooms, to it. Hack the PC and
  the guns turn on everyone but you — a target at his desk in front of a gun dies in an "accident".
- Every map has at least 8 security cameras over its gates, the corridors into restricted floors and
  the approaches to the guns, with blind spots to learn.
- New distractions everywhere: office PCs on desks, pedestal fans in kitchens and guard rooms, garden
  hoses on yard and garden walls. Each pulls the nearest guard off his post.
- More throwables (150+ per map), dropped beside guard posts and patrol stops.
- Guards walk: every floor of each key building has a patrol loop (the casa's cellar ring, portico and
  galleries; the keep's cellar, ground floor, light-well galleries and roof; every floor of the Grand
  Hotel, its roof walk and delivery yard), patrol pairs walk the plazas, market streets, souk, courts,
  chalet street and lake shore, and well over a third of the armed staff is on the move.
- Fleeing civilians have somewhere to run: each map marks its exits (docks, stations, roads, trails).
- The editor no longer crashes while building Weisshorn (cliff boulders are scene objects now), and
  steep cliffs carry a rock skin whose texture no longer streaks down the walls.
- Patrols do errands: pairs stop to chat, one has a smoke or takes a call, every other corner is a
  look around; inside, each stop fits its room (a smoke in the guard room, a watch at the vault, a lean
  on the terrace) and a patrol passing a locked door tries it. Every guarded building has someone
  walking its rooms.
- Crowds know where to run: every group of guests or staff has two ways out on opposite sides, 25-55 m
  away on open ground, besides the map's own exits (docks, stations, roads, trails).
- Staff and guests drift between break spots in every social room (the bar, the buffet, the lounge, the
  kitchen, the terrace) instead of standing on their marks all match.
- Patrol stops no longer drop to the floor below when they fall on a stair well or inside furniture.
- Buildings lose the boxy look: dressed-stone quoins on the corners of the grand houses (the hotel, the
  casa, the keep, the churches), chamfered corners on plain walls, moulded cornices and stepped string
  courses, a plinth with a projecting coping, drip moulds over the windows and stone surrounds round
  the street doors.
- Weathering: rising damp along the foot of the walls, rain streaks under window sills, hairline
  cracks, and posters and notices pasted on the town houses' street fronts.
- Shop signs you can read over the doors of the open shops and bars: a bakery, an ironmonger's, a
  billiard hall in San Lázaro (and a faded "SE ARRIENDA" on some shut houses), an épicerie, a
  pharmacie and a salon de thé in the souk, a Bäckerei, a Metzgerei, a Käserei and a Stübli on the
  chalet street.
- Roofs: ridge tiles along every pitched roof (snowed over in Weisshorn) and chimney stacks on the
  houses, chalets and the Grand Hotel.
- Real grass on the maps (the world track's GPU grass): San Lázaro's pastures and terraces, the
  Qasr oasis grove (straw tufts out in the desert), and Weisshorn's new snow-free meadows on the valley
  floor and the sunny lower slopes (the pistes, the lake shore and everything high stay white). The
  hiding fields of tall grass grow tall blades, and in Weisshorn they stand in the meadows.
- Weisshorn's ice rink has two gates: the skaters can run when trouble starts.

## Game.md notes (design / architecture)
- `RealMap` security helpers: `Route(building, (x, floor, z, height)…)` lays a cable run in building
  coordinates; `GroundRun` follows the land; `Join` concatenates; `TerminalDesk` puts the control PC on a
  desk and warns when it is not 15-40 m from its guns. Cables start at the gun's base, hug walls and
  ceilings 6 cm off the surface, pass through slabs, partitions and door heads, and end behind the PC;
  a second gun in a group daisy-chains its cable to the first.
- `ArchSpec.reserved` (floor, rect): spots the furnisher leaves free (gun pads, terminal desks).
- `ArchFurnish`: a PC distraction on every office/study/police/radio desk and the security desk; a fan
  in kitchens, guard rooms, messes, barracks and offices over 40 m²; drinks only on a placed table.
- `RealMap.TopUpThrowables(160)`: throwables 2-3.5 m beside NPC posts and patrol stops (on a floor or
  the land, never inside a wall) until the map has 160.
- `MapValidator` now requires: 150+ throwables, 8+ cameras and a recorder, 3+ sentry guns each driven
  by a terminal and reached by its cable, no gun covering a start or an extraction, 4+ fans, 3+ hoses,
  4+ PCs.
- Map notes: docs/maps/*.md section 9 (groups, terminals, routes, accident opportunities).
- Patrols go through `RealMap.Beat(name, zone, size, weapon, pause, points…)` and `Loop(building, …)`
  (building-local stops); routes are named `Route_<name>` so a richer patrol API (per-point actions)
  replaces one function. `ExitAt(position, label)` puts an `ExitPoint_<label>` marker under the
  markers and adds the AI's `ExitPoint` component by name when it exists.
- `MapValidator` adds: ≥ 35 % of armed NPCs on patrol routes, a loop (≥ 4 stops) on every floor of the
  key building that has 4+ rooms, ≥ 60 % of fans/hoses/PCs within earshot of a post or a patrol stop,
  ≥ 4 exits.
- Patrols use the AI track's placement API (`AiPlacement`, `PatrolPlanner`, `PatrolAudit`):
  `RealMap.Beat` writes a street beat's errands (chat at the first corner, a smoke or a call half-way,
  look around at every other corner), `Loop` gives each building stop its room's errand
  (`PatrolPlanner.ErrandFor`) and a `CheckDoor` near a locked door; `KeyLoops` adds a
  `PatrolPlanner.InteriorLoop` (or a pacing loop in a one-room building) to every building with 3+
  posts and no loop; `ExitAt` puts a map exit (`ExitPoint`, any zone); `CrowdExits` (after the NavMesh
  bake, `MapKit.AfterNavMesh`) clusters the crowd per zone and adds two reachable outdoor exits per
  crowd on opposite sides, public ground first.
- `MapKit.SnapToNavMesh` looks for the closest NavMesh point on the same floor first (rings up to 3.6 m,
  1 m of height), then within 4 m; activity spots are snapped too.
- `ArchFurnish.Spots`: activity nodes on free floor in social rooms (lobby/hall: idle, phone; bar:
  idle, drink; ballroom: idle, dining; salon: idle, phone; dining/mess: dining, idle; kitchen: inspect,
  idle; shop: inspect; spa, clinic, church: idle; guard room: smoke; office: phone) and smoke/idle on
  terraces, loggias and belvederes - the crowd's and the guards' break spots.
- `RealMap.CrowdSpots`: a guest or member of staff with fewer than two break spots within 25 m in its
  zone gets an idle spot and a call or a smoke 4-7 m away (snapped onto the NavMesh on its floor).
- Review shots (`MapShots`): `Review` (aerials, four times of day from a clear viewpoint, street fronts
  with posters, a dressed corner, grass at eye level), `AutoShots` (climbs, balconies, stairs, rooms),
  `SecurityShots`, and in play mode `NpcShots`, `PatrolShots`, `Panic` + `FleeShots`, `SunShot`.
- `MapValidator` runs `PatrolAudit.Fails()` and also requires 80 % of staff and guests to have two
  break spots (drink, dining, garden, smoke, phone, idle, inspect) within 30 m in their zone.
- Facade detail (`ArchBuilder`): `ArchStyle.quoins` (Fortress, stone Alpine, the hotel, the casa, the
  pueblo church) lays 0.36 m stone courses alternating long and short on each outer corner; plain
  facades get a 45-degree chamfer on outer corners (the outer leaf trimmed, one quad per corner);
  cornices are a four-step moulding, string courses three steps; the plinth gets a projecting coping;
  windows a drip mould over the lintel; street doors stone jambs, lintel and cap.
- Weathering decals (`ArchDecals`, URP `DecalProjector` with "Shader Graphs/Decal"): textures painted
  in code into Art/Generated/Decals (grime, streak, crack, three posters), rewritten only when the
  painting changes. Rising damp along every ground-floor facade in pieces of at most 5 m (every other
  one mirrored), streaks under every other upper-floor sill, a crack on about a fifth of the facade
  runs, one or two posters at eye level on bare street walls of houses whose style has `posters`. The
  projector boxes reach only 0.1-0.25 m out from the wall so passers-by are not painted; the angle fade
  keeps them off the ground and the inner faces.
- Shop signs (`ArchSigns`): `ArchDoor.sign` names a street door's sign; the text is set in the
  built-in font glyph by glyph from its atlas onto a painted board (5 colourways, picked by the text),
  saved to Art/Generated/Signs (rewritten only when the painting changes) under a URP Lit material;
  `ArchBuilder.SignBoard` stands a 4:1 wooden board (at most 2 m) on the door's lintel cap with the
  sign quad on it.
- Roofs: `ArchStyle.chimneys` (Colonial 1, Alpine 2, the hotel 4; churches and the hacienda garage 0)
  puts up to one stack per 14 m of ridge, from the roof's floor up through the slope with a cap and a
  pot; every gable or hip roof gets ridge tiles (snow on snowy styles).
- Real grass: each map's `Details()` calls `K.RealGrass(style, density, turf, dryTurf, dryness)` (the
  world track's API) instead of the textured grass detail; ferns stay details; `TallGrassDetail` is
  null. Weisshorn's `DryGrass` slot is now a meadow layer painted by `Meadow()` (below ~24 m, slopes
  under 26 degrees, not on a piste, 45 m off the lake, patchy); `RealMap.TallGrassAllowed` keeps its
  hiding fields to the meadows.
- Terrain layers: `RealMap.Own` gives each map its own copy of every layer (`TL_<id>_<scene>`), so
  another map's build no longer changes its tiling or tint.
- Build safety: `ArchMesh` skips faces with NaN or out-of-range points and caps a mesh at 1.2M
  vertices; cliff boulders are scene objects, not terrain trees (huge terrain-tree colliders sent the
  plan baker's physics queries into a lock-assert spiral that ran the editor out of memory).
