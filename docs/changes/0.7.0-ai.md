# 0.7.0 — AI (Track D)

## Changelog (player-facing)
- Guards attack again. A guard who is searching (after a shot, a lost confrontation, a radio call)
  and glimpses you comes straight at you, weapon up; he no longer calms down into a casual look.
- Guards remember. Shoot, kill, punch or take someone down in front of a guard, flee a
  confrontation, or get into a fight, and that zone knows you for two minutes: seen means shot,
  wherever you stand. Everyone else still gets the benefit of the doubt in public places.
- A gun in your hand is noticed. On the plaza, a guard walks up and tells you to put it away
  (holster it and he lets you go); anywhere else a drawn gun is a crime and guards open fire.
  Cameras see it too.
- Restricted areas: a guard warns you, then shoots - about 3-4 seconds after he first sees you.
  Hostile areas: guards shoot on sight after a short reaction. A calm, unarmed player in a public
  place is never shot.
- Gunfire carries. Guards' shots are heard like yours, and every armed guard in earshot comes
  running; real walls (not crates or fences) muffle sound, and guards see through windows.
- The last guard standing keeps shooting instead of phoning for help, and radio calls are spaced out.
- Guards aim properly while they move (no more swinging round along their path).
- Lookouts on towers see the ground below them.
- Fixes: patrols stay and look around where they went to check something; crowds run away from
  the trouble (not from the middle of the map); the target has its 120 HP.

## Design and architecture notes (for docs/Game.md)
- **Rules are pure and tested.** `AI/NpcRules` holds the brain's decisions (`AI/DetectionModel`
  the senses' numbers); `Npc` asks them. EditMode tests: `Editor/Tests/NpcRulesTests`.
- **Hunting never downgrades** (`NpcRules.CanBecomeSuspicious`/`OnEdge`): a searcher or an
  alarmed responder that glimpses a player heads for the glimpse (`Npc.HuntToward`); a full
  gauge is a fight.
- **Zone hostile memory** (`LevelZone.Hostiles`, `AI/HostileMemory`, 120 s): set by witnessed
  gunfire and explosions, kills, punches, takedowns (`Npc.ReportHostileAct`, armed witnesses
  who see the player), a surviving victim, fleeing a confrontation outside public places, a drawn
  gun where it is a crime, and any fight. A known hostile skips the tier factor and the
  confrontation (`NpcRules.OnSpotted`). Everyone else keeps the tier rules even in front of a
  searcher or a fighter (a calm passer-by is not shot because a fight goes on nearby).
- **Suspicious behaviour** = aiming, sprinting, climbing, the proof, **or a visible weapon in hand**
  (`Npc.ActsSuspicious`, `NpcRules.IsVisibleWeapon`); a weapon out outside public places is a
  crime (`NpcRules.IsCrime`) - fought without a warning.
- **Timeline**: restricted gauge factor 0.8, warning `DetectionModel.ESCORT_TIME` 1.6 s, first shot
  at the reaction time (`NpcRules.REACTION_*`); hostile tier: fight on sight.
- **Hearing**: walls are counted by `AI/Senses.WallsBetween` (steps through every surface; only
  terrain and colliders at least 1.9 m tall and 0.9 m across count; the leaves of one partition
  count once). NPC gunfire emits an alarming noise (once per 1.5 s per shooter). Every armed guard
  that hears gunfire responds (`NpcRules.AlwaysResponds`); the crowd and quieter sounds keep the
  1-3 cap; `NoiseEvent.capped` keeps the far siren capped for everyone.
- **Sight through glass**: `Senses.Clear` passes through window glass (the generator's `glass`
  batch / `M_WindowGlass`, or a `Glass` layer if the project gets one). Bullets still stop on glass.
- **Phone calls** (`NpcRules.ShouldCall`): two fighters at least, 30 s between calls (8 s after
  a cut one); a caller left alone drops the phone.
- **Lookouts** use a flat cone (`NpcRules.ConeAngle`): bearing only, 40° up to 75° down.
- `NavMeshAgent.updateRotation` is off while the NPC aims or stares (`NpcRules.AimsManually`).

## API for the map builders (levels track)
Everything is editor-side in `Assets/_Game/Editor/AI/` (namespace `Com.IsartDigital.GoobyrFPS.EditorTools`,
so the map builders see it without a `using`). Put everything under the kit's markers group
(`K.Markers`): the end-of-build NavMesh snap then moves the points onto the floor.

### Exits — where the crowd runs
```csharp
AiPlacement.Exit(K.Markers, G(-60f, -150f), 180f, ZONE_PUEBLO);   // position, yaw (out), zone (-1: any)
```
Runtime component `AI/ExitPoint`. A fleeing civilian runs for the nearest reachable exit that does
not lead toward the threat, and cowers when there is none (or the threat is too close). Put **two or
more around every crowd and public area, on opposite sides** (town gates, the road out, a door to
the street, a jetty, the far end of a square), 20-60 m from the crowd.

### Patrols with errands
```csharp
AiPlacement.Patrol(K.Markers, Z, 2, ItemKind.Smg, 3f,               // zone, size 1-5, weapon, pause (s)
    AiPlacement.At(G(-30f, -38f), PatrolAction.Chat, 10f),           // a pair stops and talks
    G(110f, -38f),                                                    // plain point: the route's pause
    AiPlacement.At(G(110f, -12f), PatrolAction.Smoke, 12f, 90f),     // errand, seconds, facing (world yaw)
    AiPlacement.Through(G(40f, -12f)),                                // walked through, no stop
    AiPlacement.At(door, PatrolAction.CheckDoor, 4f, yawToDoor));    // tries the door it faces
```
- `PatrolAction`: `Pause, LookAround, Chat, Smoke, Phone, CheckDoor, Lean, Watch`. The waypoint's
  rotation is the facing (default: the way the patrol walked in).
- Size 2 = a pair that walks side by side and chats; 3-5 = the pair plus a column behind.
- Existing routes (`A.Patrol`/`K.Route`) can get errands: `AiPlacement.Stop(route, index, PatrolAction.LookAround, 6f, yaw)`.
- Runtime: `AI/PatrolStop` on the waypoint (child of the `PatrolRoute`).

### Generated loops
```csharp
PatrolPoint[] lLoop = PatrolPlanner.InteriorLoop(_haciendaR, 0, 6);          // ArchResult, floor, max rooms [, start room]
if (lLoop != null) AiPlacement.Patrol(K.Markers, ZONE_HACIENDA, 2, ItemKind.Smg, 3f, lLoop);
AiPlacement.Patrol(K.Markers, ZONE_PUEBLO, 2, ItemKind.Pistol, 4f,
    PatrolPlanner.TownLoop(G(-40f, -150f), G(10f, -150f), G(10f, -190f), G(-40f, -190f)));
```
- `InteriorLoop`: depth-first through the doors NPCs can pass (not locked secure doors), an errand
  per room type (kitchen/mess/bar: chat; guard room/garage/store: smoke; vault/armoury/security:
  watch; terrace/loggia: lean; radio/police: phone; corridors and halls: walked through; other
  rooms: look around) and a `CheckDoor` at each locked door it passes. Returns null if the floor has
  fewer than two connected rooms.
- `TownLoop`: chat at the first corner, look around at every other one.

### Audit (validator metric)
`Goobyr/Validate/AI Patrols`, or from `MapValidator.Validate`: `lFails.AddRange(PatrolAudit.Fails());`
- at least 35 % of the guards move (on a route);
- every key building (3+ posts inside its `BuildingVolume`) has an interior loop (a route with most
  points inside it);
- at least one patrol walks the town (a route with most points in public places);
- every zone with a crowd has an `ExitPoint`; some waypoints carry errands.
