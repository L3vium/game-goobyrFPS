# 0.7.0 — World (track C: world beauty)

## Changelog (player-facing)

- **Colour grade, redone in HDR.** Calmer, cleaner contrast and colour, and a look per biome that
  holds at every hour: warm and humid jungle, golden desert, cool and crisp snow. Dawn and dusk
  are warm and bright enough to play instead of muddy brown; nights are moonlit and readable
  instead of black.
- **Living vegetation.** Trees, palms, bushes and ferns sway in one shared wind whose gusts roll
  across the land; leaves glow when the sun is behind them; detail levels cross-fade instead of
  popping.
- **Shadows on bushes, rocks and boulders.**
- **Real grass (test scene for now).** Blades of grass, not a texture: dense curved tufts that sway
  in the shared wind (gusts roll over a meadow as waves of light), glow when the sun is behind them,
  part where someone walks and stand back up, and stay pressed round dropped items so they can be
  found. The maps get it once the test scene is approved (San Lázaro first).
- **Local reflections.** Every building floor and the streets round the buildings get reflection
  probes captured at load (and again when the time of day changes): glossy floors, glass, metal and
  water reflect their surroundings instead of the sky.
- **Dawn and dusk under trees** are no longer black against the low sun (more sky light in the shade,
  warm lifted shadows).
- **A softer sky.** Rounder, billowing clouds with lit tops, soft grey bellies and bright edges round
  the sun; sky and cloud gradients no longer band.
- **Grass seen from low down** (crouching, lying in it) no longer turns black at the roots.
- **Truer colour textures.** Ground, bark, walls and props compress in BC7 on PC (no more blocky
  smears in subtle greens and browns); the armchairs' leather and wood and the packed metal maps no
  longer import washed out.
- **Ground without repeating tiles.** Grass, earth, sand and rock no longer repeat as a visible grid
  across fields and slopes, and broad patches vary their colour a little, like real land.

## Design and architecture notes

- **Grade.** `Settings/PC_RPAsset` grades in HDR (before the ACES tonemapper). The map's volume
  profile (`ArtBuilder.VolumeProfileFor`) is a calm base (the map definition's LDR-era contrast
  and saturation × 0.4) and is rewritten in place, so its GUID — and the saved map scenes —
  survive `Goobyr/Build/Materials`. `FX/TimeOfDay` grades each biome and hour on top (priority-5
  volume): `TimePreset` gained `temperature`, `tint`, `saturation`, `contrast`, `shadows`,
  `highlights` (split toning) and `wind`.
- **Presets are code, rebuilt on load.** `TimeOfDay` rebuilds its presets from
  `TimeOfDay.PresetsFor(biome, heading)` when a map loads, so a look change reaches every map
  without a map rebuild. `Setup(sun, sky, biome, heading)` stores the biome; maps built before
  0.7.0 (or through `Setup(sun, sky, presets)`) are recognised from their noon sun (62°/68°/32°).
- **Wind.** `World/Wind.cs` writes `_GoobyrWind` (direction, strength, gustiness) and
  `_GoobyrWindTime` (time, last frame's time, gust speed and size) once per frame from
  `RenderPipelineManager.beginContextRendering`, in edit mode too. `Art/Shaders/GoobyrWind.hlsl`
  holds the gust field (value-noise patches drifting downwind): grass and trees sample the same
  field, so a gust visibly travels. `Wind.Freeze` holds the wind for review shots.
- **Foliage shader.** `Goobyr/Foliage`: URP Forward+ lit (`UniversalFragmentPBR`), alpha-clipped
  two-sided leaves, translucency, ShadowCaster / DepthOnly / DepthNormals / MotionVectors passes
  (`AlwaysRenderMotionVectors`, so TAA sees the sway of objects that never move), dithered LOD
  cross-fade. It reads the Blender vertex colours (`veg_lib.py`: R ambient occlusion, G branch
  phase, B wind weight). `PbrLibrary.Foliage` switches a plant material over and `RemapModel`
  re-applies it, so rebuilding PBR materials keeps it; `_WindBend` comes from the model's height.
- **Vegetation prefabs** are tuned in place (`Goobyr/Build/Vegetation`, `VegLibrary.TuneAll`, and
  on first use by a builder): shadows from LOD0/LOD1 of every plant and rock (impostor cards of
  small plants excepted), LOD fade mode CrossFade with animated cross-fading. Terrains reference
  the prefabs by GUID, so the maps update without a rebuild.
- **Cutout textures** (leaf, frond, needle, card PNGs with alpha, and merged `_diffa`) import with
  coverage-preserving mipmaps at the material's cutoff (`PbrLibrary.CUTOFF`, 0.45).
- **Real grass** (`World/GrassField.cs`, `Art/Shaders/GrassCull.compute`, `Art/Shaders/Grass.shader`),
  stateless and GPU-procedural. The builder (`Editor/World/GrassBaker.cs`) bakes a 1024² map over the
  terrain — R density (0 on roads, lots, water, rock), G height, B dryness, A tall grass — and the
  ground's height range per 16 m chunk. For every camera (game, scene view, map shots;
  `beginCameraRendering`) the chunks within 90 m are culled on the CPU; the compute pass visits a
  0.28 m slot grid in them (hash → jitter, rank, yaw, variation), grows a tuft where the map says so,
  reads the ground from the terrain's heightmap texture, thins slots by rank with distance (the kept
  ones widen; a slot shrinks to nothing before it goes, so nothing pops), evaluates the wind and the
  trampling once per tuft (this frame and last), frustum-culls, and writes two instance buffers and
  their indirect args (counts in place, clamped). Two `Graphics.RenderMeshIndirect` draws: near tufts
  (10 curved blades, thinning to 4 on the way out) and far 4-blade cards.
- **Grass shading**: bent normals (rounded across each blade, blended with the ground's normal with
  distance), translucency, root-to-tip occlusion, per-tuft hue and brightness, the map's dryness,
  receive-only sun shadows, Forward+ additional lights (cluster loop), SSAO. Near tufts render in the
  depth-normals prepass (SSAO, early-z) and both bands in the motion-vector pass (TAA); no shadow
  caster. Under the blades and beyond 90 m the ground takes the meadow's far colour (terrain shader).
- **Trampling** (`World/GrassTrample.cs`, `GrassTrample.compute`): a 64 m window round the main camera
  (25 cm texels, half float, ping-pong) pressed by `GrassBender` components and, in play mode, by the
  players, NPCs and dropped items (found every 0.5 s); pressed grass stands back up over ~2.5 s; items
  keep a patch flat so they stay visible.
- **Review and cost**: `Goobyr/Debug/Grass Test Scene` builds `Scenes/World/GrassTest.unity`;
  `Goobyr/Debug/Grass Benchmark` plays it at 1080p (TAA, post, SSAO) from fixed viewpoints, grass on
  against grass off, and writes `Logs/grass_bench.json` (GPU frame time, per-pass GPU times, grass CPU
  time, tufts and cards drawn).
- **Sane bounds.** Procedural draws hand the culling only finite boxes within 100 km of the origin
  (`GrassField.IsSane`): the grass skips a camera at a NaN or absurd position and checks its chunk
  bounds, the snow glints skip such a camera, and trampling ignores NaN or flung sources (after the
  6000.6 editor hung on `Invalid worldAABB` / `Overflow in memory allocator` during the AI showcase).
- **Grass cost** (`Logs/grass_bench.json`, 1080p, TAA + post + SSAO, RTX 4070 Laptop, grass drawn for
  the measuring camera only, set-ups interleaved): about +0.5 ms GPU (pass sum 0.31-0.49 ms: opaque
  +0.05-0.23, depth-normals +0.09-0.10, motion vectors +0.14-0.15, cull 0.016) and 0.05 ms CPU per
  frame, 3.4-3.8k tufts + 12.3-12.8k cards (~85k blades). A GTX 1660 is about 2.3x slower (3DMark Time
  Spy graphics), so about 1.15 ms there, inside the 1.5 ms budget. Far cards in the depth prepass cost
  +0.05 ms for no gain, so only the near tufts go there; far cards keep motion vectors (no measurable
  cost).
- **Reflections** (`World/RoomProbes.cs`, added by `TimeOfDay` at runtime, so no map rebuild): one
  box-projected realtime probe per building floor (box over the floor's `SecurityZone` rooms that have
  a ceiling, captured at eye height in the largest), up to 160, plus up to 48 street probes on a 48 m
  grid near buildings; 64², HDR, world layers only, rendered two per frame at load and on a time-of-day
  change. Weisshorn: 72 probes (48 floors). Visual effect is modest on rough materials, and URP's
  diffuse ambient indoors still comes from the sky's SH: the indoor raycast dimming stays until APV.
- **Terrain pixel error 1** (`MapKit.Terrain`, applied by the next map build): slopes and banks keep
  their shape at a distance instead of faceting.
- **Grass styles and the meadow ground.** `World.GrassStyle.Jungle()/Alpine()/Oasis()`; `FarColor` /
  `FarDryColor` are the meadow's linear colour from afar (calibrated in the test scene). Each
  `GrassField` publishes its map and these colours (`_GoobyrGrassMap`, `_GoobyrGrassFar`,
  `_GoobyrGrassFarDry`, `_GoobyrGrassOn`; one field per scene) and `Goobyr/TerrainLit` paints the ground
  where grass grows in the meadow's colour, keeping the texture's detail (the mix divided by the layers'
  own average colours, read from their last mip), green to straw by the map's dryness: the land keeps
  its colour where the blades end, while lots, paths and rock painted with a grass layer but bare of
  grass keep the layer's own colour. (The first cut tinted whole layer copies, which turned grass-free
  lots into dark green felt; `GrassBaker.Untint` gave those copies their source colour back.) Grass
  maps have mipmaps, so the terrain reads them smoothly from afar.
- **Small relief** (`LandShape.Detail`, `LandShape.DetailNoise`): two octaves (8.5 m and 3.4 m, about
  ±0.2 m) added to the base relief before the stamps, so ramps and feathers stop reading as perfect
  smoothsteps while pads and roads (stamped after) stay flat; slope added stays under ~10°.
- **HDR buffer** (`ArtBuilder.InstallFeatures`, applied): 64-bit, so soft sky and cloud gradients do not
  band.
- **Sky** (`Art/Shaders/Sky.shader`, used by every map through `TimeOfDay`, no rebuild): computed in
  float. The cloud slab's shape is a normalized five-octave noise through a soft coverage ramp (no hard
  threshold), eroded at the edges by two finer octaves; it is lit from a smooth three-octave density
  toward the sun (Beer, plus a weaker second extinction for light scattered many times inside) and a
  powder term; bellies take a blue-grey ambient, tops the sky's; a Henyey-Greenstein forward lobe
  lights thin edges round the sun (`_SilverLining`). The march is jittered by interleaved gradient
  noise (per frame, so TAA averages it) and the output dithered by about half a display step. 13 noise
  lookups per step instead of 18.
- **Grass roots**: lighter root colour (`GrassStyle.root`), a root occlusion floor of 0.55 (0.8 seen
  from above), and the lower part of each blade takes the ground's normal (60 % at the root), so a low
  camera sees roots lit like the ground instead of a black comb.
- **Texture suffixes** (`PbrLibrary.IsMap`): a texture is an ARM, normal, alpha or colour map only when
  its name ends with the suffix (`_arm`, `_arm_2k`...): `FP_Arms_body_diff`, `Chr_Rook_Arms_diff` or
  `Furn_Armchair_leather_diff` are colour maps (sRGB), not ARM maps. Colour maps (`_diff`, `_diffa`)
  get a PC override to BC7 at their own size whenever their folder is prepared
  (`PbrLibrary.PrepareFolder`): the PBR library's 265 colour maps are converted, character and
  first-person folders convert on their next build (a one-time reimport, about 1 s per map). BC7 is
  8 bits per pixel against BC1's 4: opaque colour maps double in memory and in the build. The same run
  set the packed `_ms` maps linear (119 were sRGB) and packed the armchair's.
- **Terrain shader** (`Art/Shaders/Terrain/`, `Goobyr/TerrainLit`, on `M_LandPbr` through
  `MapKit.PbrLandMaterial`): URP 17.6's TerrainLit (regenerated from 17.6 sources after the Unity 6000.6 upgrade) with one change in `SplatmapMix` — each natural layer
  is also sampled at 0.27x its tiling, turned by 27°, and mixed in by a slow noise (20-65 %); the mix
  then takes the meadow's colour where real grass grows (see above) and a broad brightness and hue
  variation (patches of tens of metres). Asphalt and cobble
  (layers 7-8 in MapKit's `Ground` order) keep one scale, or a larger cobble ghosts through. The add
  pass (layers 5-8) and the basemap generation (land beyond 220 m) share the mix, so near, far and the
  extra layers match. Up to nine extra texture samples per pixel and pass (four of them from the last
  mip). No rebuild: the shared material switched shader in place.
- **No height blend.** URP's terrain height blending only works with four layers or fewer; the
  realistic maps have eight (two passes), so the mask maps' heights would be ignored. Not worth
  fetching the displacement maps.
- **APV (adaptive probe volumes with sky occlusion): no-go for 0.7.0, not baked.** It would fix the
  sky light leaking into rooms and under canopies, and sky occlusion keeps it valid at every time of
  day. Against it here: the maps are generated, so every rebuild needs a new bake (a pipeline step of
  minutes per map); switching `PC_RPAsset` to probe volumes recompiles every lit shader's variants for
  everyone; URP's bake path can open modal dialogs (no volume in the scene, several scenes open),
  which would freeze the shared editor; and the data ships in the LFS player build. Go once the maps
  freeze and the editor is free: bake San Lázaro first, measure time and size, then decide per map.
  Until then: the reflection probes per floor and `TimeOfDay`'s indoor dimming.

## Map grass: what each map builder calls (for the levels agent)

`MapKit.RealGrass(style, density, turf, dryTurf, dryness)` (in `MapKit.Terrain`, baked by `Finish`):
grass grows where `density` (0..1) allows, weighted by how much of the `turf` / `dryTurf` ground
layers is painted there. Wherever grass grows, the terrain shader paints the ground in the meadow's
colour from afar, so the land keeps its colour where the blades end at 90 m; the layers themselves
are untouched (the `<map>_Turf_<Ground>.terrainlayer` copies of the first builds are back to their
source colour, and the next rebuild stops using them: delete them then).
The tall grass fields grow 1.4-1.9 m blades (`TallGrassDetail` is ignored, the
hiding triggers stay). Remove the instanced grass detail (the old textured grass would double up);
keep ferns as details. Styles: `World.GrassStyle.Jungle()`, `.Alpine()`, `.Oasis()`.

**San Lázaro** — `MapSanLazaro.Details()` becomes:

```csharp
protected override void Details()
{
    // Real grass on the grass and dry-grass layers (replaces the Veg_Grass_Jungle detail).
    K.RealGrass(World.GrassStyle.Jungle(),
        (x, z) => Built(x, z) || K.GroundAt(x, z) < WATER_Y + 0.4f || K.LandSlope(x, z) > 34f ? 0f : Mathf.Clamp01(0.8f + 0.35f * LandShape.Fbm(x, z, 20f, 2, 9.1f)),
        new[] { Ground.Grass }, new[] { Ground.DryGrass });
    DetailPrototype lFern = VegLibrary.Detail("Veg_Fern", 0.8f, 1.4f, Color.white, new Color(0.9f, 0.95f, 0.85f));
    if (lFern != null) K.Details.Add((lFern, (x, z) => Built(x, z) || K.GroundAt(x, z) < WATER_Y + 0.4f || K.LandSlope(x, z) > 38f ? 0f : Mathf.Clamp01(Woods(x, z, K.GroundAt(x, z)) - 0.6f + LandShape.Fbm(x, z, 14f, 2, 4.4f) * 0.5f) * 1.2f, 3));
}
```

**Qasr al-Rimal** — no turf layers (the oasis ground is the desert's): the density alone decides; lush
in the grove, straw tufts outside:

```csharp
protected override void Details()
{
    K.RealGrass(World.GrassStyle.Oasis(), (x, z) =>
    {
        bool lGrove = z > -100f && z < -24f && x > -160f && x < 150f;
        if (Built(x, z) || K.LandSlope(x, z) > 30f) return 0f;
        return lGrove ? Mathf.Clamp01(0.6f + LandShape.Fbm(x, z, 15f, 2, 3f)) : Mathf.Clamp01(LandShape.Fbm(x, z, 18f, 2, 7f) - 0.15f) * 0.6f;
    }, null, null, (x, z) => z > -100f && z < -24f && x > -160f && x < 150f ? 0.25f : 0.85f);
}
```

**Weisshorn** — its Grass/DryGrass slots are snow, so meadows need a meadow layer first (a design
call for the map): e.g. `Layers()` puts `PbrLibrary.Layer("leafy_grass", 4f)` in the `DryGrass` slot
instead of `snow_field_aerial`, `NaturalGround()` paints it on the snow-free pastures (low, gentle,
sunny: `w[(int)Ground.DryGrass] = meadow * 6f`), then
`K.RealGrass(World.GrassStyle.Alpine(), (x, z) => Built(x, z) || K.LandSlope(x, z) > 30f || K.GroundAt(x, z) < 0.3f ? 0f : 1f, new[] { Ground.DryGrass });`

**Rebuild order:** San Lázaro first (MapValidator, then the world agent's G-World shots), then Qasr,
then Weisshorn once its meadows are painted. Every rebuild also takes **terrain pixel error 1** and
the **small relief** (`LandShape.Detail`, 2-10 m bumps of about ±0.2 m on the base relief, pads and
roads stay flat; `Shape.Detail = 0` turns it off for a map): props or markers placed at a fixed
height on open land rather than at `GroundAt` may float or sink by up to 0.2 m — the validator
catches them. **No rebuild** is needed for the grade, the foliage, the reflection probes, the sky,
the grass shading, the texture compression or the terrain shader.

**Status (6000.6.7 / URP 17.6, after the levels rebuilds of 3 Nov):** San Lázaro and Weisshorn draw
real grass; their `Art/Generated/Grass/Map_*_Grass.png` and `Map_*_Turf_*.terrainlayer` files are
still untracked: commit them with the map scenes (the turf copies are back to their source colour and
have mipmapped grass maps). **Qasr al-Rimal has no grass map** (`Map_QasrAlRimal_Grass.png` missing):
its `Details()` RealGrass call did not reach `Finish`, or the build was not saved; rebuild it. Weisshorn's
village lots now show the meadow texture instead of dark green felt.
