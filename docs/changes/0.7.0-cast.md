# 0.7.0 — cast (Track A)

## Changelog (player-facing)
- A new original cast is designed: the hero **Rook** (copper
  quiff, amber shades pushed up, olive bomber with an orange lining in your team colour, mustard
  scarf, cargo trousers, boots, leather gloves; male and female), three body bases and every outfit
  (jungle / desert / snow guards, bodyguard, waiter, cook, maid, mechanic, two guests, two targets).

- Faces come alive: people blink, frown when they aim at you, look scared when they flee or cower,
  wince when hit, and show X eyes when they die.
- Bodies wobble: heads and chests lag a little behind every hit, landing and sudden stop.
- Goofier ragdolls: big heads, looser joints, deaths that spin rather than fly, and still at rest within seconds.
- Hands no longer sink into bellies and hips: they slide around the body (head and torso kept clear).
- Sprinting characters may scurry with quick little steps (pace up to 2.5).
- Everyone wears the new cast: you play Rook (man or woman by seat) with your team's colour on the
  jacket lining, facings and armband; guards dress for the map, staff are waiters, cooks, maids and
  mechanics, guests wear summer clothes, and the targets are the tycoon and the magnate.
- Guards and civilians look surprised the moment they spot you.
- Feet slide less: walk, run and crouch speeds re-measured on the new bodies; arms swing clear of bellies.
- Third-person guns sit in the new gloved hands (grips measured on Rook's hand).

## Design notes (for docs/Game.md)
- **Look: TABS-flavoured, not TABS-literal.** Human proportions that keep the mocap working, a big
  readable head (0.31 m, about 1.3x), big hands, chunky brows and big eyes, smooth "premium toy"
  surfaces with real fabric, leather and metal looks. The goofiness comes from motion and faces.
- **Body metrics** (shared by every body): top of the skull 1.80 m (hair and hats extra), eyes 1.60 m
  (Core/BodyMetrics.EYE), chin 1.49 m, shoulder joints 0.36-0.40 m apart, upper arm 0.300 m,
  forearm 0.265 m, hip joints 0.90 m, knees 0.49 m. Mass sits low (belly, hips); the chest stays
  narrow at the armpits so hanging arms clear the torso.
- **One hand for everybody.** Thumb, separate index finger, fused three-finger paddle skinned equally
  to the middle / ring / pinky chains (all 30 finger bones kept). Gloves are the same hand inflated
  with a cuff; bare hands are the same mesh in skin.
- **Team colour** lives on the hero's jacket lining, front facings and armband (slots `Lining`,
  `Team`), orange by default.

## Architecture notes
- Generator: `Tools/Blender/characters/cast/` (headless Blender 5.2). Every part (head, hair, lids,
  brows, body, each garment, shoes, accessories) is a signed-distance stack (`sdf.py`) meshed with
  OpenVDB; garments are separate closed layers built on the body's lumps with an ease, and every face
  buried inside another opaque part is culled, so layers never z-fight. Specs: `cast_specs*.py`.
- Looks are Poly Haven CC0 scans (fabric, leather) recoloured per outfit and box-projected; the export
  step bakes them into `<Chr>_<Slot>_diff/_nor_gl/_arm`.
- Faces: separate head mesh; expressions (Blink, Smile, Surprised, Angry, Scared, Pain, Dead) are lid
  hinges, brow moves, jaw opening and mouth-corner moves (`faces.py`), exported as shape keys after
  rigging; X eyes on death are an eye-texture swap.
- First-person hand-off: `glove.py` (`make_fp_arm`, `bone_segments`, `frame_from_arm`) and README.md.
- Game export (`export.py`): parts decimated by visible area (hero 30k tris, NPCs 20k; boosts on face,
  hands, brows, lids), grouped by slot, unwrapped and baked from the high-res parts, the shared 53-bone
  skeleton with Chr_Agent's rolls (head bone at eye - 0.04 so HumanBody's eye normalisation stays 1),
  capsule-distance weights, the face split into `<Chr>_Head_LOD0` (+ shape keys), `_LOD1` 40 %, `_LOD2` 15 %.
  Team slots are baked grey and tinted at runtime (`HumanBody`, default orange).
- Runtime (local, cosmetic, no network): `HumanFace` (blend shapes; mood from `HumanAnimator`; `React`,
  `Die`; `HumanAnimator.Surprise()` for the AI to call when an NPC spots something), `HumanWobble` (<= 6
  springs: upper chest, neck, head; after the Animator; skipped when not seen; kicked on hits and
  landings), hand IK goals pushed out of the head sphere and a torso capsule (not for phone/smoke/takedown),
  ragdoll limbs sized from bone lengths, heavier head (<= 10:1), looser joints, spin over shove.
- `CharacterBuilder`: blend shapes imported; on the new cast the upper arms' "down" range starts 14 deg
  higher (arms stay clear of wider bodies), solid sculpted hair/brows/lashes (no alpha cards), warmer skin.
- Switch-over: `HumanCast` assigns Chr_Rook/Chr_Rook_F to players, the biome guards, Chr_Bodyguard,
  staff among Chr_Waiter/Cook/Maid/Mechanic, guests among Chr_Guest_M/F and the three bases, targets
  Chr_VIP_M or Chr_VIP_F (feminine names). The MPFB folders stay on disk unused; Chr_Agent remains the
  clips' avatar source, grips are calibrated on Chr_Rook (`CharacterBuilder.GRIP_REFERENCE`).
- `HumanAnimator` ground speeds (WALK/RUN/SPRINT/CROUCH/PANIC) come from the G2 gallery's planted-foot
  measurement on Rook; the new cast's upper-arm "down" range starts 22 deg higher.
- `NpcVisuals` calls `HumanAnimator.Surprise()` when an NPC goes from calm to suspicious/engaged/combat/flee.
- Play-mode check: `Goobyr/Cast/Showcase (San Lazaro)` (Logs/cast_showcase).
- G2 check: `Goobyr/Cast/G2 Gallery` (Editor/Pipeline/CastGallery.cs) -> Logs/cast_g2 (clip strips, grip
  close-ups, report.json); `gallery_sheet.py` makes the labelled pages.
