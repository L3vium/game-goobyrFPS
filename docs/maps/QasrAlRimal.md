# Qasr al-Rimal — desert oasis and mesas (map design, v2)

**Keywords:** audience day, wind, the fortress on the rock. **Motif:** ochre adobe with blue doors
and striped awnings, from the souk into the citadel courts. **Contrast:** the bright crowded souk
against the cool, dim citadel halls and the cold glass penthouse on top; the dust gusts against
still interiors. **Topology:** archipelago (Marrakesh template, fixed: ≥ 5 entries per fortress,
two secret links, a social layer inside the citadel). Terrain 500 × 500 m, play space ~380 × 380 m.
Borders: dune sea (S), canyon walls (N, E), salt flat and a ruined caravan road (W).

## 0. Systems (same rules as every map)
Security tiers with access (*Guest*: audience petitioners; *Staff*: kitchens, press, motor pool;
*Security*: offices, war room, penthouse, radar); passes (Petition token, Staff pass, Keycard);
enforcers see through passes; escort before shooting in Restricted; crowbar and keycard doors;
overheard chatter as guidance. **Wind:** every 3-4 min a dust gust (40 s) cuts NPC sight range by
half outdoors — a timed opening the player reads from the sky and the sound.

## 1. Zones and the snail

```
 N canyon walls ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                     THE NEEDLE (rock pinnacle +60 m: overlook onto the court and the terrace)
  [C] CITADEL on the Great Mesa (+42 m)              [D] RADAR OUTPOST, Little Mesa (+38 m)
   gate court split in 3 courts · keep (4 fl.)  ◄─ rope bridge ─►  bunker · dish gantry · berms
   ▲ switchbacks (4 hairpins) · cliff chimney + scaffolding · well shaft · goods lift   ▲ track · ladder face
  [B] PALM GROVE & WADI (−4 m): irrigation channels fed by the spring pool, date press with a
      donkey-driven press, reed beds, rock arch, abandoned kasbah halfway up the switchbacks
   ▲ footbridges, two qanat branches
  [A] AIN ZARQA town (0 m): spring pool + well plaza, two covered souk streets, tea house,
      caravanserai, mosque + minaret, bus station; ~60 houses (16 enterable), roof planks
 S dune sea ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Spiral:** bus station → souk → well plaza → grove paths → wadi → switchbacks wrap the mesa → the
three courts climb in terraces to the keep → keep roof → helipad. The Little Mesa joins by the rope
bridge (west bastion) or from the wadi by a track and a ladder face. **Water that makes sense:**
the spring pool in the town feeds the grove's channels (gravity, downhill to the wadi); the qanat
is a **dry, ancient smugglers' tunnel** (two branches) ending at the foot of the mesa in a
**rock-cut well shaft** with ladders and landings up to the citadel cistern (filled by rain and a
pump). Secret link 2: the **smugglers' cleft** from the radar bunker to the citadel cellars.
**No dead ends:** souk alleys loop, both qanat branches rejoin at the shaft, the cleft has two mouths.

**Legitimacy sources:** *Petition token* — the clerk at the bus station hands them out, or take one
from a petitioner in the tea house; *Staff pass* — cook (kitchen larder), press foreman, motor-pool
mechanic, each alone beside a hiding spot; *Keycard* — the treasurer and the colonel. **Enforcers:**
the governor's guard (see through Guest), the colonel's adjutant (sees through Staff).

## 3. Landmarks, overlooks, light, sightlines

- **Hero:** the citadel keep with its glass penthouse and helipad beacon, 60 m over the town.
- **Secondary:** minaret (A), date press and the rock arch (B), radar dome (D), the Needle.
- **Overlooks that work (level or downward):** the **Needle** (reached by a cliff path from the
  wadi) sees down into the first court and the penthouse terrace (governor's tea), not the bedroom;
  the **abandoned kasbah** roof sees the switchbacks and the gate; the **radar dish gantry** sees
  the helipad at the same height. Each is seen from another.
- **Sightlines:** long views only over the souk roofs and the dunes. The mesa face is broken by
  gullies, a rock chimney, restoration scaffolding and cliff caves (rest points). The courts are
  split into three (stable block, parked trucks, delivery queue, covered colonnade, carpets drying
  on lines). The radar mesa has sandbag berms, container stacks and the dish gantry. Inside the keep
  the light well is screened by carved wooden mashrabiya screens and columns.
- **Light:** white glare and saturated awnings in the souk; green shade in the grove; dim halls cut
  by god rays from slit windows; cold marble penthouse (personal); daylight shafts in the qanat.
- **Wayfinding:** the call to prayer, the press's donkey bell, blue doors on public passages, signs.

## 4. Key building — the Citadel keep

34 × 34 m, 4 storeys + cellar, around a screened light well; inside three courts (110 × 80 m in all).

```
CELLAR (−3.6): cistern landing (well shaft) · armoury · generator+breaker · AIR HANDLER · vault (PERS) · cleft door
GROUND (0):    gate hall + GRAND STAIR · guard room · kitchen + SERVICE stair · mess hall · stores · linen room (breather)
FLOOR 1 (3.6): AUDIENCE HALL (chandelier, petitioners) · waiting gallery · offices ×3 · archive · monitoring room · library
FLOOR 2 (7.2): war room (PERS) · officers' quarters ×4 · baths · gallery over the light well
FLOOR 3 (10.8): penthouse — salon, bedroom (PERS), study, terrace + pool · helipad roof above
```

**28 enterable rooms**, two exits each (toilets excepted); every floor loops around the light well
plus an inner loop of connecting doors. **Grand stair** (gate hall → floor 2), **service stair**
(cellar → roof), goods-lift shaft (climbable), a **secret stair from the library (public audience
floor) to the war room** (public → private). **Vent:** air handler → service core → war room →
penthouse → roof grille. Audience day: first court + gate hall + audience hall + waiting gallery are
Guest; offices Staff; floors 2-3 and the vault Security.

## 5. Secondary fortresses
- **Radar outpost (D):** bunker (−1 command, −2 generators), dish gantry, motor pool, fuel tanks.
  Entries: front gate (Security), motor-pool ramp (Staff), ladder face from the wadi (climb), cleft
  tunnel (underground), dome vent (climb). Opportunity: the fuel tanks; the rotating dish sweeps the
  gantry railing.
- **Date press (B):** press house (2 floors), stores, donkey yard. Entries: yard gate, loading door,
  loft hatch via palm trunk, channel culvert. Signature machine: **the screw press**.
- **Caravanserai (A):** two-level gallery court (loop), stables, upper rooms; entries: gate, stable
  door, roof planks, well.

## 7. Opportunities, items, guidance
Audience-hall chandelier; air handler gas; breaker; the screw press; poisonable tea (tea house,
penthouse terrace) with **pharmacy pills in the souk** and **rat poison in the stables** beside
them; fuel tanks; the pool terrace's loose glass pane; fireworks at the market stage. Crowbar on the
scaffolding, keycards with the treasurer / colonel / lift operator, petition tokens. Throwables:
pottery and bottles (souk), rocks (wadi, qanat), wrenches (motor pool). Hiding: laundry baskets,
carpet piles, crates, date sacks, reeds along the channels; disposal in the cistern, the press and
the canyon drop. **Chatter:** petitioners grumble about the lift operator's keycard, cooks about the
governor's tea, technicians about the fuel leak, the archivist about the library's "draughty shelf".

## 8. Pacing, starts, multiplayer, reuse
- **Starts (6)** each forking into ≥ 3: bus station (souk / qanat mouth in the dyers' alley / roof
  planks), caravan road (caravanserai / grove / town wall), dune edge (qanat exit / grove / wadi),
  grove ford (press / wadi / switchbacks), wadi arch (Needle path / well shaft / radar track),
  canyon trail (radar ladder face / cleft mouth / Needle).
- **Intensity:** souk → grove (breather) → courts (professional) → kasbah or cistern landing
  (breather) → audience hall (public rule) → penthouse (peak).
- **Multiplayer:** ≥ 3 routes per start cluster; qanat has two branches and the shaft has two
  ladders; alerts stay in their zone and cool down after 2 min; shared shortcuts by design.
- **Reuse:** the souk is cover, a chase and an extraction (bus); the wadi is a route, a sniper duel
  (Needle vs gantry) and a disposal site; the audience hall is a social approach, a chandelier kill
  and an escape through the library stair.
- **Extractions:** helicopter (radar helipad), car (caravan road), bus (station), qanat to the dunes.
