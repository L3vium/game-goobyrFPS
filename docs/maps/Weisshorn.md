# Weisshorn — alpine gala in the snow (map design, v2)

**Keywords:** gala night, snow, the lodge on the ridge. **Motif:** dark timber and stone under heavy
snow, amber windows, from the village chalets to the hotel. **Contrast:** the glittering two-level
ballroom against the boiler underbelly and a cold-war bunker. **Topology:** onion with a festival
core — the gala is a public-rule spine **inside** the hotel, reached legally from the village by the
guest funicular. Terrain 500 × 500 m, play space ~360 × 360 m. Borders: glacier and summit (N),
avalanche cliffs (E), frozen lake and pine wall (SW).

## 0. Systems
Security tiers with access (*Guest*: gala invitation; *Staff*: kitchens, ski room, lift stations;
*Security*: suites wing, security office, bunker); passes (Gala invitation, Staff pass, Keycard);
enforcers see through passes; escort before shooting in Restricted; crowbar and keycard doors;
overheard chatter. **Snowfall** varies (light / heavy): heavy snow halves NPC sight outdoors.

## 1. Zones and the snail

```
 N glacier · summit ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [D] OBSERVATORY & BUNKER (+70 m): dome, antenna huts, rock outcrops, snow tunnel, bunker −1/−2
        ▲ cable car (keycard)       ▲ ridge path between outcrops
  [C] GRAND HOTEL WEISSHORN (+45 m): 3 tiers in the slope; behind it a 6 m cut retaining wall
      and a crevasse (the roof is NOT reachable from the snow behind except by entry #9)
        ▲ guest funicular (Guest)   ▲ service road switchbacks   ▲ ski pistes
  [B] SKI AREA (+10 → +40 m): lift station, mid-station hut, piste huts, ski-jump tower, groomer
      garage; pine bands and snow fences between runs (open runs ≤ 40 m)
        ▲ chairlift   ▲ footpaths
  [A] SANKT ANNA (0 m): chalet street (28 chalets, 10 enterable), church, Gasthaus, ski-rental,
      lake rink, funicular valley station, boathouse
 S frozen lake ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Spiral:** bus stop → chalet street → valley station / chairlift → pistes and the service road
wrap the slope → the hotel's tiers step up the mountain (each tier has its own terrace at ground
level) → ridge path / cable car → observatory. **Secret links:** the old **mine gallery** (groomer
garage ↔ hotel cold store) and the **bunker escape tunnel** (bunker ↔ under the ski-jump).
**No dead ends:** piste paths loop through the mid-station, the mine gallery has two mouths, every
hotel floor is a ring.

## 3. Landmarks, overlooks, light, sightlines
- **Hero:** the hotel **clock tower** beside the lit **ski-jump tower**; amber windows read through
  snowfall from >75 % of the exterior. Rooms that cannot be entered keep curtains drawn and dim
  light; enterable floors glow brightly.
- **Secondary:** observatory dome (D), church spire (A), lift-station wheel house and mid-station (B).
- **Overlooks:** ski-jump platform → ballroom terrace and the presidential balcony (not the bedroom);
  church spire → funicular top platform; dome catwalk → helipad. Contested pairs.
- **Sightlines:** long views only along the chalet street and over the lake. Pistes are broken by
  pine bands, snow fences, piste huts on the overlook lines and the mid-station; runs ≤ 40 m open.
  The observatory ridge has rock outcrops, antenna huts and a snow tunnel. Climbables are dark timber
  and black iron, readable against snow at night.
- **Light:** warm amber interiors as beacons; coloured piste markers; blue moonlit snow; harsh
  fluorescent underbelly; red bunker. **Wayfinding:** the gala band, the boiler's thud, piste signs,
  the funicular bell.

## 4. Key building — Grand Hotel Weisshorn

70 × 30 m, three tiers + suites floor + attic, clock tower.

```
UNDERBELLY (0, lower terrace): boiler room · generator + breaker · AIR HANDLER · cold store (mine door) · laundry · coal chute · lockers
STAFF TIER (4, ski-room terrace): kitchens + SERVICE stair · ski room · security office (monitoring) · canteen · wine cellar + DUMBWAITER · linen room (breather) · goods lift
GUEST TIER (8, front terrace): lobby + GRAND STAIR · BALLROOM floor (chandelier ×2, stage) · bar · spa (pool, sauna) · library · smoking terrace (breather)
MEZZANINE (10.5): ballroom balcony (public rule) brushing the staff catwalk over the kitchens · private cinema (PERS)
SUITES (12): 8 suites on a ring corridor, south balconies linked by a continuous ledge · presidential suite (PERS)
ATTIC (16): attic store · clock-tower stair · roof walk · helipad
```

**30 enterable rooms**, two exits each (toilets excepted); every floor is a ring around the atrium
or service core. **Grand stair** (lobby → suites), **service stair** (underbelly → attic), goods lift
shaft, clock-tower spiral. **Secret passage public → private:** the library bookcase opens onto the
dumbwaiter shaft linking the wine cellar, the private cinema and the presidential suite pantry.
**Vent:** boiler air handler → service core → ballroom ceiling → presidential suite → roof grille.

## 7. Opportunities, items, guidance
Two ballroom chandeliers; air handler gas; breaker; champagne tower and suite minibar with village
pharmacy pills and **rat poison in the cold store** beside them; sauna overheating; groomer tiller;
chairlift bullwheel; the ski-jump's icy platform rail; cable-car maintenance lever. Crowbar in the
groomer garage, padlock key with the mechanic, keycards with the security chief and the operator,
invitations at the Gasthaus. Throwables: bottles (bar, kitchen), rocks and ice chunks (pistes),
wrenches (garage, boiler). Hiding: laundry carts, lockers, wardrobes, ski racks, frosted reeds on the
lake shore; disposal in the lake hole, the crevasse, the groomer. **Chatter:** waiters on the
chandelier rigging, the chef on the champagne, instructors on the mine gallery, technicians on the
cable-car fault.

## 8. Pacing, starts, multiplayer, reuse
- **Starts (6)** each forking into ≥ 3: bus stop (funicular / chairlift / Gasthaus), boathouse (lake
  culvert / rink / chalet roofs), lake shore (reeds / service road / boathouse), chairlift bottom
  (lift / pistes / groomer garage), pine copse (south ledges / mid-station / ski-jump), glacier trail
  (ridge path / crevasse / observatory).
- **Intensity:** village → boathouse or copse (breather) → ski area (professional) → mine hut
  (breather) → ballroom (public rule) → suites (peak).
- **Multiplayer:** ≥ 3 routes per start cluster; two underground links; alerts stay in their zone and
  cool down after 2 min; shared shortcuts by design.
- **Reuse:** the chalet street is cover, a chase and an extraction (snowmobile); the pistes are a
  route, a sniper duel (ski-jump vs spire) and an accident (groomer); the ballroom is a social
  approach, a chandelier kill and an escape through the library passage.
- **Extractions:** helicopter (roof), cable car (observatory), snowmobile (village), funicular.
